# World Window QA fixes — September 14, 2026

Rollback checkpoint: `a85cbe7bd656a5493d761bf7e4324a22a5d4a58b`, tagged `checkpoint/world-window-before-qa-20260914`.

## Implemented

- Travel reads now respect account and request ordering. Older detail responses cannot overwrite newer history or reintroduce a trip removed by a later refresh. Open details follow the current provider record.
- Dreams refreshes are serialized. Header and body reads have a 30-second deadline. Duplicate simultaneous edits are blocked; failed/uncertain writes retain local records and explain how to reconcile. Close and Android Back remain usable during saves. Original captions are retained; damaged summaries use recoverable punctuation or known place metadata for display.
- Failed secure credential deletion has a retry flow and a token-free pending-cleanup marker that blocks silent rehydration. Gmail scan status is distinct from archive refresh and no longer always claims a connection is working.
- Visited tabs and collection origins remain mounted, preserving trip search/filter/scroll and nested collection position. Back returns to the actual origin. Inactive layers cannot receive touches or appear in accessibility trees. A hidden Home year picker cannot cover an incoming Dreams share.
- Year-filtered countries follow the selected flights. Airport counts include recorded airports without coordinates. Country links match canonical travel-country identities; connections count and Somaliland remains distinct. Flight dates retain their recorded calendar day.
- Pinch-to-one-finger dragging continues naturally. Globe accessibility actions support rotation, zoom, route/country browsing, and clearing selection. Repeated paths reuse geometry while preserving individual flight IDs. Hidden/background globes stop animation and evict detail textures; resume preserves zoom and rotation.
- Passport identity and record pages expose their visible information to accessibility. Collection headers, sprite cropping, large-text layouts and year-axis labels are corrected. The physical cover, folds, chronological stamps, and existing theme remain intact.
- Fixed safe-area backgrounds protect the status bar. Actual Newsreader italic replaces synthetic fallback. Wallet previews are denser and show the destination and return across connections; all original flights remain in detail. Empty Dreams maps frame the known country without inventing place pins. Profile prioritizes scan status, distinguishes its two refresh actions, and places atlas labels clear of markers.

## Data and release scope

The fixes change presentation and client request handling. No live flight, trip, account, or saved-place records were edited, imported, deleted, or reassigned for this work. Preservation and failure cases use synthetic fixtures. This is not a fresh reconciliation of every historical booking.

Full persisted offline archive support remains the separate follow-up identified in the plan. iOS, device-fleet performance, native TalkBack gestures, physical multitouch, and memory-pressure behavior require device coverage beyond the offline tests. Android artifact and connected-device verification results are recorded in the final delivery report.

## world-window

Reference: window-current.png

Use case: logo-brand. Asset: ONE high-resolution professional brand design exploration BOARD, a single square artboard containing exactly NINE original Trotter monogram logo studies arranged in a beautifully spaced 3 by 3 grid. This is a comparative visual sheet for a travel app founder. The supplied reference is the app's CURRENT mark, a starting point for an inventive family of variations; do not just repeat it 9 times. Make 9 related but clearly different silhouettes. Drawing quality of an exceptional midcentury identity designer: optically balanced shapes, deliberate negative space, impeccable curves, simple memorable marks that would work at 24 pixels and in one ink. Flat precise clean vector-like drawing, generous margins, no clipping, no shadows, gradients, texture, mockups, 3D, watermark, generic airplane/compass clipart, or copied airline identity. All nine symbols equally prominent. NO wordmark under each symbol. Only the small board title at the top and the specified short ID beneath each logo. Each cell has ample empty paper space. Very faint hairline separators between cells allowed. No captions or invented filler. Draw the logos large and fill the sheet elegantly; keep each whole logo comfortably inside its own cell. 2048x2048 or higher resolution.
Board title exactly: "TROTTER — WORLD WINDOW". Palette: uniform cloud ivory #FAF8F2 background, Atlantic blue #427494 marks. A restrained terracotta #C37453 satellite dot is permitted on W03 and W08 only. Everything else blue.
Design family: worldly 1950s jet-age optimism, a clearly legible T monogram with ONE elegant elliptical flight orbit. The orbit must really wrap in front of and behind the T using clean negative-space crossing gaps; its satellite dot must sit ON the path. NO extra outer circle, no enclosing circular badge, no globe grid, no planetary ball. This is a letter and an orbit, not Saturn. Vary structure and typography rather than just moving the dot. Preserve no flaws or awkward joins from the reference.
Read grid left-to-right top-to-bottom:
W01: Refined classic — confident straight geometric T, balanced moderately tilted narrow ellipse and small dot, highly resolved minimal evolution of reference.
W02: Swept crossbar — T top has elegantly upturned tapered ends like a very subtle wing; long slim orbit around its lower half, no dot. Airy horizontal silhouette.
W03: Soft modernist — broad friendly rounded-square T proportions, restrained round corners, steeper spacious orbit, one tiny terracotta dot at top right.
W04: Sculpted serif — beautifully flared subtle wedge-serif T, not ornate, wrapped by a light inclined ellipse, no dot. Cultivated printed travel identity.
W05: Integrated path — a broad smooth continuous band makes an identifiable T whose crossbar develops into its orbital loop; clean one-piece clever construction, no dot, much less literal than W01.
W06: Upright meridian — tall elegant T and an oblique elongated ellipse tilted around 60 degrees, clearly passes behind crossbar and in front of lower stem; a tiny blue satellite; sophisticated vertical silhouette.
W07: Open orbit — compact sturdy T, orbit made from one tapering open near swoop plus spare far arc; intentional generous openings, no enclosing circle, no dot; most reductive.
W08: Horizon — very wide slender T with a softly arched top and shallow long lower orbit; one small terracotta point riding far right. Space-age print elegance, not a flying saucer.
W09: Bold cut — chunky T with one thick elliptical band, negative-space ivory gap where band crosses, no dot; decisive solid simplified small-icon alternative.
Only labels W01 W02 W03 W04 W05 W06 W07 W08 W09, each centered beneath its logo.

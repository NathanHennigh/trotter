## Shared generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Design ONE new iteration for Trotter, a cultured personal travel archive with an elegant midcentury airline / continental travel identity.
Input images are user-selected earlier logo concepts, visual references for this new iteration. Preserve the characteristic economical swept/tapered shapes, generous negative space, ivory-paper palette with petrol/navy and muted golden yellow. Follow the specific design changes below to make this a visibly distinct new design.
Rendering: beautifully proportioned flat logo, crisp vector-like clean solid shapes, one decisive idea, legible as a small app icon. Opaque, UNIFORMLY SOLID warm ivory #F4EFDF canvas. Truly solid-color shapes: no mottling, paper texture, highlights, gradients, shading, dimensional effects, or distressed edges. Simple expressive geometry; no photoreal anatomy, faces, fingers, shoe details, lettering, numbers, watermark, diagrams, mockups or multiple versions.
Composition: exactly one complete mark centered in a square canvas with generous margin, logo occupies about 66% width. No wordmark. Refine the actual silhouette rather than just recoloring it.

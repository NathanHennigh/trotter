# World Window: experience and polish review

Reviewed 14 September 2026 against mobile source at `64e0424`, with the latest available saved Android captures and subsequent layout proofs. This is a review, not an implementation. The phone was unavailable through ADB during this review. Findings below distinguish source-confirmed behavior, design proposals, and interactions requiring another physical-device check. Google map layout proofs contain a placeholder, so they do not establish native map appearance or performance.

## Recommended direction

Keep World Window's restrained blue, cream, and copper palette and its passport, wallet, and photographic postcard objects. The next improvement should make navigation, feedback, and gesture behavior feel as considered as those objects. The priorities are consistent actions, reliable state, and continuity between an object and its detail screen.

Existing nested Android Back handling, retained tab state, hidden-screen accessibility, chronological stamps, two-page passport spreads, rigid cover, edge taps, fully expanded flight tickets, and globe texture blending are present. These should not be treated as missing features or reopened as old bugs.

## Motion and crafted details

These are design proposals, not claims that the device currently drops frames.

| Before | After | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The globe already blends textures and dims unrelated routes smoothly, while the surrounding ticket/country card mounts instantly. | Coordinate a short ticket entrance with the existing route fade, then carry its airport codes/date into the trip detail. Retain reduced-motion alternatives and interruptibility. | Motion should explain where content came from, not delay access. Timing is a starting point to tune on the phone. |

Additional touches worth prototyping:

- **Trips:** strengthen the hierarchy between the wallet, journey legs, and flight tickets. Make dates and connection information quieter but easier to scan. Keep every flight expanded. Use existing data to show clear day changes and connections; do not invent gates, seats, or ticket classifications.
- **Passport:** refine the identity page as a printed document with compact field labels, deliberate alignment, and integrated rules. Keep chronological ordering and natural stamp placement with safe margins. A newly earned country stamp could settle into the book once after a successful import; existing pages should not replay a ceremony on every visit.
- **Dreams:** combine the repeated country/header/count layers into one useful heading. Keep category/city filtering within easy reach when the collection contains dozens of restaurants, cafes, and hotels. Preserve natural photography and a consistent crop, paper border, and typography across country images and reel-cover fallbacks.
- **Collections:** retain simple lists and small square airline logos. Give countries an arrival-led row, airports an IATA-led row, and airlines a logo-led row, with consistent tap behavior. A country detail can include its relevant trips directly rather than adding another browsing mode.
- **Profile:** the Gmail section is already above the airport illustration. Keep that improvement. Give the airport heading a link to its existing collection detail, so the illustration has a purpose. Reduce label collisions in dense route fans; prioritize selected/important labels instead of presenting every label at equal weight.
- **Haptics:** use a restrained optional tick for a completed page turn, a newly selected chart year, or a confirmed location. Trigger it on the meaningful state change, never continuously while dragging.
- **Return cues:** render the small existing return-origin label beside collection Back arrows. It currently reaches accessibility labels but is not visible, which is less clear when Globe opens a Passport collection.

## Physical-device checks still required

1. Passport starts pointer capture and asks the parent to lock scrolling on pointer-down. Verify whether a vertical gesture beginning on the cover traps scrolling to collections/activity below. If so, discriminate scroll intent before committing to a turn while retaining the user's ability to grab a page naturally. [Runtime](C:/Users/natha/projects/trotter/mobile-v2/src/components/world-window/passport/passport-runtime.ts:119).
2. The activity chart selects a year on every touch move without direction discrimination. Verify vertical scrolling over the chart; horizontal scrubbing should not compete with page scrolling. [Chart](C:/Users/natha/projects/trotter/mobile-v2/src/components/world-window/passport/ActivityChart.tsx:24).
3. Airport/airline collection index and detail share an unkeyed ScrollView. Confirm opening a detail starts appropriately and Back restores the list position. Source structure alone does not establish native offset behavior. [Collections](C:/Users/natha/projects/trotter/mobile-v2/src/components/world-window/passport/PassportCollections.tsx:84).
4. Verify actual Google tiles, marker rendering, pin selection, first-location confirmation, map/list gesture arbitration, and camera behavior on Android. Offline layout proofs cannot establish these.
5. Test rapid reversal/cancellation of passport and postcard gestures, repeated Back during loading, large text, and long real itineraries. Do not declare motion polished based only on still screenshots.

## Suggested sequence

1. Correct action targets, year scope, draft protection, authentication retry, and scan continuity.
2. Simplify map/location confirmation and make trip navigation consistent.
3. Resolve gesture conflicts on the phone.
4. Add coordinated ticket/postcard transitions and shared press feedback.
5. Refine paper, typography, labels, and a few meaningful haptics.

No app source, account settings, backend data, or deployments changed during this review.
